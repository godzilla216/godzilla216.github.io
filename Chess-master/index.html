THE SHORT VERSION

Welcome to the table.

Two people share this board. White moves first. Select a piece, then select one of its highlighted legal squares.

  • The goal: checkmate the opposing king, leaving it under attack with no legal escape.
  • Special moves: castling, en passant, and pawn promotion are all included.
  • Draws: stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition, and the 50-move rule end the game automatically here.
  • Keyboard: use Tab to reach the board, arrow keys to explore, and Enter or Space to select. Escape clears your selection.

Use Undo for a second thought, or Flip board for a new perspective. Your game is saved in this browser automatically.
